About

Argus is an autonomous orbital analyst that watches the Earth around the clock. It plans its own observations, acquires and analyzes the data, and verifies its conclusions before anything reaches a human — every alert traces back to raw imagery, and the pipeline is built so no single source can fabricate one on its own.
