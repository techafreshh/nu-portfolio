About

decision-cortex is a company brain built over 2,309 Rust RFCs. Answers carry per-sentence citations so every claim points at its source, faithfulness scoring checks the model against what the corpus actually says, and abstention receipts are issued when the honest answer is "the RFCs don't cover this" — instead of a confident guess.
